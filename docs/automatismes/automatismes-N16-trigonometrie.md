## Série A — Cercle trigonométrique : valeurs de base

<p class="auto-meta">⏱ 6 min · Calculatrice interdite</p>

<div class="auto-consigne" markdown>
**Consigne.** Convertir et lire les coordonnées des angles remarquables.
</div>

<ol class="auto-questions">
<li value="1">Combien de degrés représentent <span class="arithmatex">\(\pi\)</span> radians ?</li>
<li value="2">Combien de degrés représentent <span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\)</span> radians ?</li>
<li value="3">Écrire <span class="arithmatex">\(60^\circ\)</span> en radians.</li>
<li value="4">Calculer <span class="arithmatex">\(\cos(0)\)</span>.</li>
<li value="5">Calculer <span class="arithmatex">\(\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)\)</span>.</li>
<li value="6">Donner <span class="arithmatex">\(\cos\left(\dfrac{\pi}{6}\right)\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{6}\right)\)</span>.</li>
</ol>

??? tip "Coup de pouce"

    <p><strong>Conseils</strong></p><ul><li><span class="arithmatex">\(180^\circ=\pi\)</span> radians.</li><li>Sur le cercle unité, le point associé à <span class="arithmatex">\(x\)</span> a pour coordonnées <span class="arithmatex">\((\cos x;\sin x)\)</span>.</li><li>Retenir les angles <span class="arithmatex">\(0,\dfrac{\pi}{6},\dfrac{\pi}{4},\dfrac{\pi}{3},\dfrac{\pi}{2}\)</span>.</li></ul>
    <p><strong>Vigilance</strong></p><ul><li>Le cosinus est l'abscisse.</li><li>Le sinus est l'ordonnée.</li></ul>

??? success "Corrigé de la série A"

    <ol class="auto-corrige">
    <li value="1"><span class="arithmatex">\(\pi\)</span> radians correspondent à <span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{180^\circ}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="2"><span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\)</span> radians correspondent à <span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{90^\circ}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="3"><span class="arithmatex">\(60^\circ=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{\dfrac{\pi}{3}}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="4"><span class="arithmatex">\(\cos(0)=\boxed{1}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="5"><span class="arithmatex">\(\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{1}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="6"><span class="arithmatex">\(\cos\left(\dfrac{\pi}{6}\right)=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}}\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{6}\right)=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{\dfrac12}\)</span>.</li>
    </ol>
    <div class="auto-methode"><strong>Méthode.</strong> Toujours relier un angle au point du cercle unité : abscisse = cosinus, ordonnée = sinus.</div>
    <div class="auto-vigilance"><strong>Vigilance.</strong> Ne pas inverser les valeurs de <span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{\pi}{6}\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\)</span>.</div>

## Série B — Angles associés et signes

<p class="auto-meta">⏱ 8 min · Calculatrice interdite</p>

<div class="auto-consigne" markdown>
**Consigne.** Utiliser les symétries du cercle trigonométrique.
</div>

<ol class="auto-questions">
<li value="1">Donner un angle de <span class="arithmatex">\(\left]-\pi;\pi\right]\)</span> associé à <span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{5\pi}{3}\)</span>.</li>
<li value="2">Calculer <span class="arithmatex">\(\cos\left(\dfrac{5\pi}{3}\right)\)</span>.</li>
<li value="3">Donner un angle de <span class="arithmatex">\(\left]-\pi;\pi\right]\)</span> associé à <span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{7\pi}{4}\)</span>.</li>
<li value="4">Calculer <span class="arithmatex">\(\sin\left(\dfrac{7\pi}{4}\right)\)</span>.</li>
<li value="5">Calculer <span class="arithmatex">\(\cos\left(-\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\right)\)</span>.</li>
<li value="6">Calculer <span class="arithmatex">\(\sin\left(-\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\right)\)</span>.</li>
</ol>

??? tip "Coup de pouce"

    <p><strong>Conseils</strong></p><ul><li>Ajouter ou retrancher <span class="arithmatex">\(2\pi\)</span> ne change pas le point du cercle.</li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(\cos\)</span> est pair : <span class="arithmatex">\(\cos(-x)=\cos x\)</span>.</li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(\sin\)</span> est impair : <span class="arithmatex">\(\sin(-x)=-\sin x\)</span>.</li></ul>
    <p><strong>Vigilance</strong></p><ul><li>Le signe dépend du quadrant.</li><li>Réduire l'angle avant de calculer.</li></ul>

??? success "Corrigé de la série B"

    <ol class="auto-corrige">
    <li value="1"><span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{5\pi}{3}-2\pi=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(-\dfrac{\pi}{3}\)</span>, donc <span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{-\dfrac{\pi}{3}}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="2"><span class="arithmatex">\(\cos\left(\dfrac{5\pi}{3}\right)=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\cos\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{\dfrac12}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="3"><span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{7\pi}{4}-2\pi=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(-\dfrac{\pi}{4}\)</span>, donc <span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{-\dfrac{\pi}{4}}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="4"><span class="arithmatex">\(\sin\left(\dfrac{7\pi}{4}\right)=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\sin\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="5"><span class="arithmatex">\(\cos\left(-\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\right)=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\cos\left(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\right)=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{-\dfrac12}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="6"><span class="arithmatex">\(\sin\left(-\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\right)=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(-\sin\left(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\right)=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}}\)</span>.</li>
    </ol>
    <div class="auto-methode"><strong>Méthode.</strong> Réduire un angle permet de retrouver une valeur remarquable et son signe.</div>
    <div class="auto-vigilance"><strong>Vigilance.</strong> La parité du cosinus et l'imparité du sinus ne doivent pas être confondues.</div>

## Série C — Équations trigonométriques simples

<p class="auto-meta">⏱ 8 min · Calculatrice interdite</p>

<div class="auto-consigne" markdown>
**Consigne.** Résoudre sur l'intervalle <span class="arithmatex">\([0;2\pi[\)</span>.
</div>

<ol class="auto-questions">
<li value="1">Résoudre <span class="arithmatex">\(\cos x=\dfrac12\)</span>.</li>
<li value="2">Résoudre <span class="arithmatex">\(\sin x=-1\)</span>.</li>
<li value="3">Résoudre <span class="arithmatex">\(\cos x=0\)</span>.</li>
<li value="4">Résoudre <span class="arithmatex">\(\sin x=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\)</span>.</li>
<li value="5">Résoudre <span class="arithmatex">\(\cos x=-\dfrac12\)</span>.</li>
<li value="6">Combien de solutions a généralement <span class="arithmatex">\(\cos x=a\)</span> avec <span class="arithmatex">\(-1&lt;a&lt;1\)</span> sur <span class="arithmatex">\([0;2\pi[\)</span> ?</li>
</ol>

??? tip "Coup de pouce"

    <p><strong>Conseils</strong></p><ul><li>Placer d'abord la valeur sur l'axe correspondant.</li><li>Chercher les points du cercle ayant cette abscisse ou ordonnée.</li><li>Donner les angles dans l'intervalle demandé.</li></ul>
    <p><strong>Vigilance</strong></p><ul><li>Pour <span class="arithmatex">\(\cos x=a\)</span>, on lit les abscisses.</li><li>Pour <span class="arithmatex">\(\sin x=a\)</span>, on lit les ordonnées.</li></ul>

??? success "Corrigé de la série C"

    <ol class="auto-corrige">
    <li value="1"><span class="arithmatex">\(\cos x=\dfrac12\)</span> donne <span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{\text{$x=\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ ou $x=\dfrac{5\pi}{3}$}}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="2"><span class="arithmatex">\(\sin x=-1\)</span> donne <span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{x=\dfrac{3\pi}{2}}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="3"><span class="arithmatex">\(\cos x=0\)</span> donne <span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{\text{$x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ ou $x=\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$}}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="4"><span class="arithmatex">\(\sin x=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\)</span> donne <span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{\text{$x=\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ ou $x=\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$}}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="5"><span class="arithmatex">\(\cos x=-\dfrac12\)</span> donne <span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{\text{$x=\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$ ou $x=\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$}}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="6">En général, il y a <span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{2}\)</span> solutions sur <span class="arithmatex">\([0;2\pi[\)</span>.</li>
    </ol>
    <div class="auto-methode"><strong>Méthode.</strong> Une équation trigonométrique se lit géométriquement sur le cercle unité.</div>
    <div class="auto-vigilance"><strong>Vigilance.</strong> Les bornes de l'intervalle comptent : ici <span class="arithmatex">\(2\pi\)</span> est exclu.</div>

## Série D — QCM : trigonométrie

<p class="auto-meta">⏱ 6 min · Calculatrice interdite</p>

<div class="auto-consigne" markdown>
**Consigne.** Choisir la seule réponse correcte.
</div>

<ol class="auto-questions">
<li value="1"><span class="arithmatex">\(\cos(\pi)\)</span> vaut<ol class="auto-choix" type="A"><li><span class="arithmatex">\(1\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(0\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(-1\)</span></li></ol></li>
<li value="2"><span class="arithmatex">\(\sin(\pi)\)</span> vaut<ol class="auto-choix" type="A"><li><span class="arithmatex">\(0\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(1\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(-1\)</span></li></ol></li>
<li value="3"><span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{3\pi}{2}\)</span> correspond à<ol class="auto-choix" type="A"><li><span class="arithmatex">\(180^\circ\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(270^\circ\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(360^\circ\)</span></li></ol></li>
<li value="4"><span class="arithmatex">\(\cos\left(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)\)</span> vaut<ol class="auto-choix" type="A"><li><span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac12\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\)</span></li></ol></li>
<li value="5"><span class="arithmatex">\(\sin\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{6}\right)\)</span> vaut<ol class="auto-choix" type="A"><li><span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac12\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(-\dfrac12\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\)</span></li></ol></li>
<li value="6">Un angle associé à <span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{11\pi}{6}\)</span> dans <span class="arithmatex">\(\left]-\pi;\pi\right]\)</span> est<ol class="auto-choix" type="A"><li><span class="arithmatex">\(-\dfrac{\pi}{6}\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{\pi}{6}\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{5\pi}{6}\)</span></li></ol></li>
</ol>

??? tip "Coup de pouce"

    <p><strong>Conseils</strong></p><ul><li>Revenir au cercle unité.</li><li>Contrôler le quadrant pour le signe.</li><li>En QCM, une valeur remarquable suffit souvent.</li></ul>
    <p><strong>Vigilance</strong></p><ul><li><span class="arithmatex">\(\pi\)</span> radians ne valent pas <span class="arithmatex">\(360^\circ\)</span>.</li><li>Soustraire <span class="arithmatex">\(2\pi\)</span> conserve le point.</li></ul>

??? success "Corrigé de la série D"

    <ol class="auto-corrige">
    <li value="1"><strong class="auto-resultat">Réponse C</strong> : <span class="arithmatex">\(\cos(\pi)=-1\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="2"><strong class="auto-resultat">Réponse A</strong> : <span class="arithmatex">\(\sin(\pi)=0\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="3"><strong class="auto-resultat">Réponse B</strong> : <span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{3\pi}{2}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(270^\circ\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="4"><strong class="auto-resultat">Réponse A</strong> : <span class="arithmatex">\(\cos\left(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac12\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="5"><strong class="auto-resultat">Réponse B</strong> : <span class="arithmatex">\(\sin\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{6}\right)=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(-\dfrac12\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="6"><strong class="auto-resultat">Réponse A</strong> : <span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{11\pi}{6}-2\pi=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(-\dfrac{\pi}{6}\)</span>.</li>
    </ol>
    <div class="auto-methode"><strong>Méthode.</strong> La trigonométrie rapide repose sur trois réflexes : réduire, placer, lire les coordonnées.</div>
    <div class="auto-vigilance"><strong>Vigilance.</strong> Un angle associé n'est pas seulement une valeur proche : il doit différer d'un multiple de <span class="arithmatex">\(2\pi\)</span>.</div>
