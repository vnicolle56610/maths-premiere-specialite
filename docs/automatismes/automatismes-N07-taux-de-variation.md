## Série A — Réactivation : fonctions, calculs et coordonnées

<p class="auto-meta">⏱ 6 min · Calculatrice interdite</p>

<div class="auto-consigne" markdown>
**Consigne.** Répondre rapidement. Garder les résultats exacts.
</div>

<ol class="auto-questions">
<li value="1">Calculer <span class="arithmatex">\(f(2)\)</span> pour <span class="arithmatex">\(f(x)=3x^2-5x+1\)</span>.</li>
<li value="2">Calculer <span class="arithmatex">\(g(-1)\)</span> pour <span class="arithmatex">\(g(x)=-2x+7\)</span>.</li>
<li value="3">Développer et réduire <span class="arithmatex">\((x-3)^2\)</span>.</li>
<li value="4">Résoudre <span class="arithmatex">\(2x+6=0\)</span>.</li>
<li value="5">Calculer <span class="arithmatex">\(y_B-y_A\)</span> pour <span class="arithmatex">\(A(1;4)\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(B(5;12)\)</span>.</li>
<li value="6">Calculer <span class="arithmatex">\(x_B-x_A\)</span> pour <span class="arithmatex">\(A(1;4)\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(B(5;12)\)</span>.</li>
</ol>

??? tip "Coup de pouce"

    <p><strong>Conseils</strong></p><ul><li>Remplacer <span class="arithmatex">\(x\)</span> par la valeur donnée.</li><li>Respecter l'ordre choisi.</li><li>Développer avec une identité remarquable.</li></ul>
    <p><strong>Vigilance</strong></p><ul><li>Attention aux parenthèses pour <span class="arithmatex">\(x=-1\)</span>.</li><li>Une différence d'ordonnées n'est pas une pente.</li></ul>

??? success "Corrigé de la série A"

    <ol class="auto-corrige">
    <li value="1"><span class="arithmatex">\(f(2)=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(3\times2^2-5\times2+1=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(12-10+1=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{3}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="2"><span class="arithmatex">\(g(-1)=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(-2\times(-1)+7=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(2+7=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{9}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="3"><span class="arithmatex">\((x-3)^2=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(x^2-6x+9=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{x^2-6x+9}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="4"><span class="arithmatex">\(2x+6=0 \Longleftrightarrow\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\( 2x=-6 \Longleftrightarrow\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\( x=\boxed{-3}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="5"><span class="arithmatex">\(y_B-y_A=12-4=\boxed{8}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="6"><span class="arithmatex">\(x_B-x_A=5-1=\boxed{4}\)</span>.</li>
    </ol>
    <div class="auto-methode"><strong>Méthode.</strong> Pour préparer la pente, on calcule séparément l'accroissement vertical et l'accroissement horizontal.</div>
    <div class="auto-vigilance"><strong>Vigilance.</strong> La pente sera un quotient : <span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}\)</span>, pas seulement <span class="arithmatex">\(\Delta y\)</span>.</div>

## Série B — Taux de variation : calcul direct

<p class="auto-meta">⏱ 7 min · Calculatrice interdite</p>

<div class="auto-consigne" markdown>
**Consigne.** Calculer le taux de variation demandé.
</div>

<ol class="auto-questions">
<li value="1">Pour <span class="arithmatex">\(f(x)=2x+1\)</span>, calculer le taux de variation entre <span class="arithmatex">\(1\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(4\)</span>.</li>
<li value="2">Pour <span class="arithmatex">\(f(x)=x^2\)</span>, calculer le taux de variation entre <span class="arithmatex">\(1\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(3\)</span>.</li>
<li value="3">Pour <span class="arithmatex">\(f(x)=x^2-2x\)</span>, calculer le taux de variation entre <span class="arithmatex">\(0\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(2\)</span>.</li>
<li value="4">Pour <span class="arithmatex">\(f(x)=\dfrac{1}{x}\)</span>, calculer le taux de variation entre <span class="arithmatex">\(1\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(2\)</span>.</li>
<li value="5">Pour <span class="arithmatex">\(f(x)=3x^2+1\)</span>, calculer <span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{f(2)-f(0)}{2-0}\)</span>.</li>
<li value="6">Pour <span class="arithmatex">\(f(x)=5-2x\)</span>, calculer le taux de variation entre <span class="arithmatex">\(-1\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(3\)</span>.</li>
</ol>

??? tip "Coup de pouce"

    <p><strong>Conseils</strong></p><ul><li>Formule : <span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a}\)</span>.</li><li>Calculer <span class="arithmatex">\(f(a)\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(f(b)\)</span>.</li><li>Simplifier le quotient à la fin.</li></ul>
    <p><strong>Vigilance</strong></p><ul><li>Ne pas inverser numérateur et dénominateur.</li><li>Il faut <span class="arithmatex">\(a\neq b\)</span>.</li></ul>

??? success "Corrigé de la série B"

    <ol class="auto-corrige">
    <li value="1"><span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{f(4)-f(1)}{4-1}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{9-3}{3}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{2}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="2"><span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{f(3)-f(1)}{3-1}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{9-1}{2}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{4}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="3"><span class="arithmatex">\(f(0)=0\)</span>, <span class="arithmatex">\(f(2)=0\)</span>, donc <span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{0-0}{2-0}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{0}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="4"><span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{f(2)-f(1)}{2-1}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{\frac12-1}{1}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{-\frac12}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="5"><span class="arithmatex">\(f(2)=13\)</span>, <span class="arithmatex">\(f(0)=1\)</span>, donc <span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{13-1}{2}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{6}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="6"><span class="arithmatex">\(f(-1)=7\)</span>, <span class="arithmatex">\(f(3)=-1\)</span>, donc <span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{-1-7}{3-(-1)}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{-2}\)</span>.</li>
    </ol>
    <div class="auto-methode"><strong>Méthode.</strong> Le taux de variation entre <span class="arithmatex">\(a\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(b\)</span> mesure l'accroissement moyen de <span class="arithmatex">\(f\)</span> par unité d'abscisse.</div>
    <div class="auto-vigilance"><strong>Vigilance.</strong> Le signe du taux donne le sens global de variation entre deux abscisses, pas toute la variation de la fonction.</div>

## Série C — Pente d'une sécante : changement de registre

<p class="auto-meta">⏱ 7 min · Calculatrice interdite</p>

<div class="auto-consigne" markdown>
**Consigne.** Associer calcul, points et interprétation graphique.
</div>

<ol class="auto-questions">
<li value="1">Calculer la pente de la droite passant par <span class="arithmatex">\(A(1;2)\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(B(4;8)\)</span>.</li>
<li value="2">Calculer la pente de la droite passant par <span class="arithmatex">\(C(-2;5)\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(D(2;-3)\)</span>.</li>
<li value="3">La pente vaut <span class="arithmatex">\(3\)</span>. Si <span class="arithmatex">\(x\)</span> augmente de <span class="arithmatex">\(2\)</span>, de combien <span class="arithmatex">\(y\)</span> augmente-t-il ?</li>
<li value="4">Pour <span class="arithmatex">\(f(x)=x^2+1\)</span>, déterminer la pente de la sécante entre <span class="arithmatex">\(0\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(3\)</span>.</li>
<li value="5">Pour <span class="arithmatex">\(f(x)=4-x^2\)</span>, déterminer la pente de la sécante entre <span class="arithmatex">\(-1\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(2\)</span>.</li>
<li value="6">Si le taux de variation de <span class="arithmatex">\(f\)</span> entre <span class="arithmatex">\(2\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(6\)</span> vaut <span class="arithmatex">\(-3\)</span>, calculer <span class="arithmatex">\(f(6)-f(2)\)</span>.</li>
</ol>

??? tip "Coup de pouce"

    <p><strong>Conseils</strong></p><ul><li>Pente : <span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{y_B-y_A}{x_B-x_A}\)</span>.</li><li>Pour une courbe, les ordonnées sont les images.</li><li>Lire une pente comme un quotient.</li></ul>
    <p><strong>Vigilance</strong></p><ul><li>Le dénominateur est un accroissement d'abscisse.</li><li>Une pente négative signifie que la droite descend de gauche à droite.</li></ul>

??? success "Corrigé de la série C"

    <ol class="auto-corrige">
    <li value="1"><span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{8-2}{4-1}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{6}{3}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{2}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="2"><span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{-3-5}{2-(-2)}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{-8}{4}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{-2}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="3"><span class="arithmatex">\(\Delta y=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(3\times2=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{6}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="4"><span class="arithmatex">\(f(0)=1\)</span>, <span class="arithmatex">\(f(3)=10\)</span>, donc <span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{10-1}{3-0}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{3}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="5"><span class="arithmatex">\(f(-1)=3\)</span>, <span class="arithmatex">\(f(2)=0\)</span>, donc <span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{0-3}{2-(-1)}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{-1}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="6"><span class="arithmatex">\(f(6)-f(2)=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(-3\times(6-2)=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\boxed{-12}\)</span>.</li>
    </ol>
    <div class="auto-methode"><strong>Méthode.</strong> Une pente de sécante est un taux de variation lu géométriquement.</div>
    <div class="auto-vigilance"><strong>Vigilance.</strong> Sur une courbe, la pente de la sécante dépend des deux points choisis.</div>

## Série D — Type épreuve anticipée : QCM

<p class="auto-meta">⏱ 6 min · Calculatrice interdite</p>

<div class="auto-consigne" markdown>
**Consigne.** Une seule réponse correcte. Ne pas justifier.
</div>

<ol class="auto-questions">
<li value="1">Le taux de variation de <span class="arithmatex">\(f\)</span> entre <span class="arithmatex">\(a\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(b\)</span> est :<ol class="auto-choix" type="A"><li><span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{b-a}{f(b)-f(a)}\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a}\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(f(b)-f(a)\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{f(a)}{f(b)}\)</span>.</li></ol></li>
<li value="2">Pour <span class="arithmatex">\(f(x)=x^2\)</span>, le taux de variation entre <span class="arithmatex">\(2\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(5\)</span> vaut :<ol class="auto-choix" type="A"><li><span class="arithmatex">\(3\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(7\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(9\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(21\)</span>.</li></ol></li>
<li value="3">La pente de la droite passant par <span class="arithmatex">\(A(0;3)\)</span> et <span class="arithmatex">\(B(4;11)\)</span> vaut :<ol class="auto-choix" type="A"><li><span class="arithmatex">\(2\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(4\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(8\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{11}{4}\)</span>.</li></ol></li>
<li value="4">Si un taux de variation est négatif entre deux abscisses, alors :<ol class="auto-choix" type="A"><li>la fonction est toujours décroissante ;</li><li>l'accroissement moyen est négatif ;</li><li>les images sont toujours négatives ;</li><li>les abscisses sont négatives.</li></ol></li>
<li value="5">Pour <span class="arithmatex">\(f(x)=2x-5\)</span>, le taux de variation entre deux abscisses distinctes vaut toujours :<ol class="auto-choix" type="A"><li><span class="arithmatex">\(-5\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(2\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(2x-5\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(0\)</span>.</li></ol></li>
<li value="6">Si <span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{f(4)-f(1)}{4-1}=5\)</span>, alors <span class="arithmatex">\(f(4)-f(1)\)</span> vaut :<ol class="auto-choix" type="A"><li><span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac53\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(5\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(15\)</span></li><li><span class="arithmatex">\(20\)</span>.</li></ol></li>
</ol>

??? tip "Coup de pouce"

    <p><strong>Conseils</strong></p><ul><li>Identifier la formule avant de calculer.</li><li>Éliminer les réponses impossibles.</li><li>Garder le lien : taux = pente.</li></ul>
    <p><strong>Vigilance</strong></p><ul><li>Une information moyenne ne décrit pas forcément tout l'intervalle.</li><li>Attention aux phrases trop fortes.</li></ul>

??? success "Corrigé de la série D"

    <ol class="auto-corrige">
    <li value="1"><strong class="auto-resultat">Réponse B</strong> : <span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a}\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="2"><strong class="auto-resultat">Réponse B</strong> : <span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{25-4}{5-2}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{21}{3}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(7\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="3"><strong class="auto-resultat">Réponse A</strong> : <span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac{11-3}{4-0}=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(\dfrac84=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(2\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="4"><strong class="auto-resultat">Réponse B</strong> : l'accroissement moyen est négatif.</li>
    <li value="5"><strong class="auto-resultat">Réponse B</strong> : une fonction affine <span class="arithmatex">\(x\mapsto mx+p\)</span> a pour taux constant <span class="arithmatex">\(m\)</span>.</li>
    <li value="6"><strong class="auto-resultat">Réponse C</strong> : <span class="arithmatex">\(f(4)-f(1)=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(5\times(4-1)=\)</span><wbr><span class="arithmatex">\(15\)</span>.</li>
    </ol>
    <div class="auto-methode"><strong>Méthode.</strong> En QCM, chercher d'abord la formule ou l'interprétation attendue, puis calculer seulement ce qui est nécessaire.</div>
    <div class="auto-vigilance"><strong>Vigilance.</strong> Une pente moyenne négative ne prouve pas que la fonction décroît partout entre les deux abscisses.</div>
